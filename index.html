Focus Tower — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Focus Tower ("the game", "we") is made by Klarity Labs. This policy explains what information the game collects when you play, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Klarity Labs. Contact: yonenotion107@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in and play, we store:

  • Account details: your email address and the basic profile your Google account shares when you choose "Continue with Google" (your name and profile picture address). Sign-in is handled by our sign-in provider and Google; we never see your Google password.
  • Display name: the name other players see on brackets and leaderboards.
  • Your planner: the quests, habits and schedule you enter, so they are available when you sign in and so the game can check tasks and habits you complete.
  • Game progress: currencies, upgrades, heroes, Focus sessions and their timing, tournament entries, rankings, album and event progress.
  • Technical data: your IP address and request times in short-lived server logs, used for security, abuse prevention and fixing errors.
  • Discord (only if you link it): your Discord user id, for the optional Discord features.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and the game you asked to play (sign-in, saving progress, tournaments and leaderboards).
  • To keep the game fair and secure (preventing cheating, abuse and duplicate accounts).
  • To fix problems and keep the service running.

The legal basis is that this processing is necessary to provide the game you signed up for, and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

What other players see

Your display name, rank frame, league, tournament results and leaderboard values are visible to other players. Your email address and planner are never shown to anyone.

Where it is stored and who helps us

  • Supabase (sign-in and database), hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google (sign-in with your Google account), under Google's own privacy policy.
  • DigitalOcean (game server and database backups), hosted in the United Kingdom (London).
  • itch.io distributes the game download under its own privacy policy.

These providers process data only on our behalf. Where data leaves the EU/UK, it is protected by the providers' standard contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your planner, display name and leaderboard entries are deleted immediately and your sign-in is removed; anonymous game records needed to keep past tournament results consistent remain without anything that identifies you. Backups containing your data are overwritten within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 14 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in the game: Account → DELETE ACCOUNT.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask questions: email yonenotion107@gmail.com.
  • If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Focus Tower is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell players in the game or on the itch.io page.
